# Simple place information template

This plain-language draft is for the team to share information about the
places they recommend. It is not a technical app or database form. The
development team will organize the information and prepare it for the app.
The team can suggest, remove, or add details to the template.

The CSV sheets below can be opened in Google Sheets or Excel. Add one row per
place in the **Places** sheet. Photos are optional; add one row per place with
photos in the **Photos** sheet. You may send several image files for one place.

## Places sheet

| Column | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Shop or place name | The name people know the establishment by. |
| Category | For example: tambayan or study spot, coffee shop, park or open space, karinderya or affordable eatery, or other. |
| Area or nearby landmark | For example, Malinta, near PLV Annex, or near CPAG. |
| Address or location details | If known. Please do not guess an exact address. |
| Google Maps link or coordinates | Optional. You can paste a Google Maps link using the place's **Share** option. If you already have coordinates, include them too; no need to find coordinates if you are unsure. |
| Short description | A few words about the place. English or Filipino is fine. |
| Usual price or spending per person | If known. It is okay to leave blank if unsure; please do not guess. |
| Operating days and hours | For example: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM; Sunday closed. Write “Not sure” for hours that have not been confirmed. |
| Amenities | For example: free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, or electrical outlets. |
| Indoor or outdoor | Indoor, outdoor, both, or not sure. |
| Suitable for | For example: studying, eating, relaxing, or meeting friends. |
| Other helpful details | Optional information the team thinks students should know. |

Example (fictional):

| Shop or place name | Category | Area or nearby landmark | Usual price or spending per person | Operating days and hours | Amenities | Indoor or outdoor | Suitable for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sample Study Cafe | Coffee shop | Near PLV Annex | Around ₱80–₱200 | Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM; Sunday closed | Free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, outlets | Indoor | Studying, eating |

## Photos sheet

| Column | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Shop or place name | Use the establishment's name so the photos can be matched to the place. |
| Photos or shared folder | Attach or share as many photos as you have for this place. If using a shared drive, a folder named after the place is fine. No special order is needed. |
| Photo-use permission | Please say whether the photos are okay to share and use in the app, or whether permission still needs to be checked. |

You can also send the original image files separately, grouped into folders
named after the places. Please only share photos that the team has permission
to provide for the app; flag any photos whose permission is unclear.

## Helpful reminders

- Unknown details can be left blank or marked “Not sure.” Please do not guess.
- For hours, “Closed” means the place is known to be closed that day; “Not
  sure” means the hours have not been confirmed.
- Prices are optional. Only write “Free” when that has been confirmed.
- The current app does not have a complete capacity or spaciousness filter. If
  you have that information, include it under “Other helpful details” for
  discussion.
- The current app uses sample listings for demonstration. Real information
  will be reviewed and checked before it replaces the sample data.
